SOLAR PANEL GUN — Prototype

Click to lock mouse and play.

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · Ctrl crouch

1 Solar Panel Gun · 2 Cable Gun · 3 Cable Router · 4 Inverter Gun

Solar Panel Gun: Left Click fire a panel (snaps into a grid next to existing panels) · Right Click pick one up · R reload

Place 100 panels to unlock the Area Tool: hold Right Click on a start panel/surface, look toward the far corner, release to build the whole rectangle at once

Cable Gun: Left Click a panel or inverter to start a run, click again to route each waypoint or click another panel/inverter to finish · Right Click finish at the last point, or cancel/remove a run. Runs automatically bend at corners to hug the surface — roof, then down the wall, like real conduit.

Cable Router: once a run exists, hold Left Click on it, look to where you want it to bend, release to route it around an obstacle · Right Click a bend to straighten it back out

Inverter Gun: Left Click mounts a 3kW inverter on a wall (wider grid spacing than panels) · on a tier-0 unit, Right Click picks it up; three adjacent tier-0 units auto-combine into a 10kW unit. On a bigger unit, Right Click instead selects it — highlight 3 same-tier big units anywhere to combine them again (10kW→20kW→50kW). Each inverter shows its kW rating and running kWh-produced sign.

Wire a panel array to an inverter with the Cable Gun (you can connect several separate arrays into one inverter — their kW stacks), then press E on the inverter to switch it on — powered cables show a faint spark running down to the inverter, and its sign ticks up a live "connected/capacity" kW reading plus a running kWh-produced total. Exceeding the connected capacity — whether at power-on or by wiring more in afterward while it's already running — will overload it: the inverter, its chained partners, and the whole array catch fire, burn, and the cables fall apart.

Cable two inverters directly together to chain them — a heavier orange 16mm flex cable, not the usual red/black pair — and their kW capacity pools into one shared budget across the whole chain.

Panels touching each other in a grid combine their wattage into one block automatically — no cable needed between them — so one cable from anywhere in a 100-panel block carries the whole block's total to the inverter. Its sign shows load as a percentage: green under 50%, yellow 51-85%, red 86-97%, flashing red 98-100%, and past 100% it overloads.

Fire is dangerous and spreads. An overloaded inverter and its array stay on fire (and stay live/electrified) until you deal with it — left alone for 30 seconds, the fire spreads to everything else on the same building and along every cable it's connected to, even into unrelated arrays, potentially setting a whole cabled-together district alight.

Give 1000 cable scrap + 500 panel scrap to the salvage cleric (walk up close to them at the Salvage Yard) to unlock the Water Gun (5). Switch a burning inverter off first (E), then hold Left Click to spray it and its panels out. Spraying anything still live destroys it and electrocutes you (respawn) — operational cables are safe to hit, but a burning, still-live cable is fatal too.
