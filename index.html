SOLAR PANEL GUN — Prototype

Click to lock mouse and play.

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · Ctrl crouch

1 Solar Panel Gun · 2 Cable Gun · 3 Cable Router · 4 Inverter Gun

Solar Panel Gun: Left Click fire a panel (snaps into a grid next to existing panels) · Right Click pick one up · R reload

Place 100 panels to unlock the Area Tool: hold Right Click on a start panel/surface, look toward the far corner, release to build the whole rectangle at once

Cable Gun: Left Click a panel or inverter to start a run, click again to route each waypoint or click another panel/inverter to finish · Right Click finish at the last point, or cancel/remove a run. Runs automatically bend at corners to hug the surface — roof, then down the wall, like real conduit.

Cable Router: once a run exists, hold Left Click on it, look to where you want it to bend, release to route it around an obstacle · Right Click a bend to straighten it back out

Inverter Gun: Left Click mounts a 3kW inverter on a wall (wider grid spacing than panels) · on a tier-0 unit, Right Click picks it up; three adjacent tier-0 units auto-combine into a 10kW unit. On a bigger unit, Right Click instead selects it — highlight 3 same-tier big units anywhere to combine them again (10kW→20kW→50kW). Each inverter shows its kW rating and running kWh-produced sign.

Wire a panel array to an inverter with the Cable Gun, then press E on the inverter to switch it on — powered cables show a faint spark running down to the inverter, and its kWh sign ticks up over time. Wiring more array wattage than the inverter's kW rating and switching it on will overload it — the inverter and array catch fire, burn, and the cable falls apart.
